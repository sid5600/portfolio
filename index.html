The Method

Four phases. No shortcuts.

Every engagement follows the same disciplined arc — adapted to the project's scale, never collapsed. The phases below are the skeleton; the documented cases show what lives inside each one.

Understand

Auditing what exists, interviewing stakeholders, and mapping the competitive landscape before forming a single opinion.

Audit Research Stakeholder Interviews

Define

Distilling research into a clear strategic brief — the single document every design decision answers back to.

Strategy Brief Principles

Make

Iterative design with a bias toward early physical or digital output — nothing is too rough to show if it moves the conversation forward.

Iteration Prototyping Review

Transfer

Handing over a system, not just files — with documentation thorough enough that the client's team can evolve it without losing coherence.
